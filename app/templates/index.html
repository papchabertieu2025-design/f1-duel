Grid or model?

Before a Formula 1 race, can machine learning predict the finishing order better than the starting grid?

Model B: the grid

Drivers finish where they start. No learning: the rule to beat.

Model A: machine learning

A softmax ranking model that also knows each driver's pace and the strength of his car.
